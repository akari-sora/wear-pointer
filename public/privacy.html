Privacy Policy — Wear Pointer

Application: Wear Pointer (dev.wearpointer)
Effective Date: November 15, 2026
Developer: Yushang Lung

1. Summary — Zero Data Collection

Wear Pointer does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, analytics, identifiers, or telemetry to external servers. The Wear OS application does not request the Android INTERNET permission and contains zero third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2. Sensor & Bluetooth Data Usage

  • Motion Sensors (Gyroscope & Gravity): Inertial sensor readings (up to 100 Hz) are processed solely in volatile memory on your watch to compute relative wrist orientation angles for controlling your own computer's mouse pointer.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Used exclusively to send short (≤ 20-byte) real-time pointer movement, click, and scroll notifications directly over a local Bluetooth Low Energy GATT link to your nearby desktop receiver. Bluetooth permissions are declared with neverForLocation and are never used to derive physical location.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service: Used solely to keep the active spatial pointer session alive while you control your computer and to provide an immediate Stop action on your wrist.

3. Local Storage

Wear Pointer stores no personal accounts, credentials, or cloud backups (android:allowBackup="false"). Session state exists only in memory while the app is running and is discarded when you tap Stop or close the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Wear Pointer is a general-purpose desktop peripheral utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Open Source & Verification

Wear Pointer is open-source software licensed under the MIT License. You can inspect the complete Wear OS and desktop receiver source code at https://gitlab.com/aa22396584/wear-pointer.
